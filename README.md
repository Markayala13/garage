## 🏆 **About Maple Motorsports**

Founded in 2025 by veteran racer and entrepreneur Michael Maples, Maple Motorsports is a full-time competitor in the NASCAR ARCA Menards Series. Based in Oklahoma, our team combines championship-level talent with cutting-edge technology and an unwavering commitment to excellence.
